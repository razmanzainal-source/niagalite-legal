NiagaLite Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

NiagaLite is an offline-first business manager. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves the device, and what it never collects.

Short version: NiagaLite does not have accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking. Your business data stays on your device. The developer does not receive it.

1. Information stored on your device

NiagaLite stores the information you enter: business name, owner name, phone, email, address, logo, products, product photos, customers, orders, payments, stock movements, packing checklists, settings and generated reports. This information is stored locally in the app's private storage (SQLite and image files) and is not transmitted to the developer.

2. Permissions

  • Photos / Camera: used only when you choose an image for a product or business logo. Images are copied into the app's private storage.
  • Files: used only when you choose to export, share or restore a .nlight backup.
  • NiagaLite does not request contacts, location, microphone, health or tracking permission.

3. Sharing and backups

Receipts, catalogs, reports and backups leave the app only when you explicitly use the system share sheet or export function. Backups can be protected with an AES-256 passphrase. You are responsible for where you store or send them. The developer cannot open an encrypted backup without the passphrase.

4. Purchases and support

On iOS, optional tips are consumable In-App Purchases processed by Apple. NiagaLite receives only the purchase result; it never receives your card or Apple Account details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

On Android, the optional DuitNow QR code is only an image. Payment happens inside your banking app; NiagaLite does not process or store the transaction.

Tips and donations unlock no content and are entirely voluntary.

5. No advertising, analytics or accounts

NiagaLite contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking, login, cloud database or remote server. The developer does not collect personal data from the app.

6. Security

Data is protected by your device's operating-system security. Optional encrypted backups use AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation. No method is completely secure; keep your device passcode and backup passphrase safe.

7. Data deletion

You can delete products, customers and orders inside the app. Deleting the app removes its local database and images. Backups or exported files remain wherever you saved them; delete them there. The developer holds no copy.

8. Children

NiagaLite is a business tool intended for adults and small-business operators. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
